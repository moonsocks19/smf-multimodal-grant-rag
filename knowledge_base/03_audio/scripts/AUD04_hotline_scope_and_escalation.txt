Before we connect you to an advisory officer, please note what this advisory service can and cannot do. We can explain the eligibility criteria of Enterprise Singapore and Workforce Singapore support schemes. We can explain what costs each scheme supports and excludes. We can walk you through the Business Grants Portal submission process, and we can review your documents before you submit them. We cannot tell you that your company is eligible or that your application will be approved. Only the administering agency can decide that. We cannot submit or manage an application on your behalf, because third party application and management is not permitted under these schemes. We cannot advise on tax matters, customs duties, or company structuring. We cannot help you present your financial position differently in order to pass an assessment. If you ask us something outside this scope, our officers will tell you plainly that they cannot help, and where possible they will point you to the right agency instead. Please also understand that support rates, caps and deadlines change, and twenty twenty six is an unusually busy year for scheme changes. Where our officer is not certain that a figure is current, they will say so, and they will check the official page with you rather than quote a number from memory. A wrong figure is worse than no figure, because members make commitments on the strength of what we tell them. Finally, if your enquiry does not name a specific scheme, or if we do not yet know your sector, your company size, or your ownership, our officer will ask you a few questions first. Those details change the answer materially, so please bear with us. Thank you. Connecting you now.
