This is Novara Microelectronics, an electronics assembly member. Their surface mount lines were producing defects that the naked eye could not catch, and no off-the-shelf software could predict them. The company needed a model built from its own inspection data. The Enterprise Development Grant funded the AI build, and an A*STAR T-Up research secondment brought in the expertise they did not have in house. Now defects are flagged before they ever leave the line.
