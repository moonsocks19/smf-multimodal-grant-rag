This is Tanjung Precision Works, a precision machining shop that was losing tenders not on quality but on speed. Job scheduling ran on spreadsheets. Quoting took three days. Machines sat idle every night shift. They started with a Productivity Solutions Grant pre-approved scheduling system. Then a custom project let the machines run unattended overnight. Quoting is now same day.
